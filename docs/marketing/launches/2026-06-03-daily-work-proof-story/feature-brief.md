## Story

Someone asks: "Did you actually get enough done today?"

Normally you would dig through chats, docs, commits, browser tabs, notes, and calendar events to reconstruct the day.

KnowYou changes the conversation: it collects the work context already on your Mac and turns it into a local daily memory. Instead of defending yourself with vague claims, you can show a timeline, source evidence, todos, and a summary of what happened.

The emotional hook is not surveillance. It is relief: your day was real, and now it is readable.

## User Pain

- Busy work days are fragmented across apps.
- It is hard to remember and explain what actually happened.
- Managers, teammates, clients, or even your future self may ask for a status update after the context has already scattered.
- AI agents also start from zero unless you manually reconstruct the day.

## User Value

- See a local timeline of the day.
- Turn scattered context into a readable daily memory.
- Use source evidence to verify what happened.
- Produce a clearer status update or weekly review.
- Give AI agents trusted context without uploading everything to a cloud memory service.
